## No usable station (checked 2026-11-14)

NOAA's ISD lists no station within 60 km of the Liwa box that overlaps 2000-2024 by at least 3 years except Shawalah (ISD 410160, Saudi Arabia, 59 km S), and within 100 km only Medina Zayed (ISD 412260, 83 km WNW). Both have a handful of reports in 2001-2003 and nothing usable; Buhasa (99 km) ended in 1997. Any national (UAE National Center of Meteorology) stations in the Liwa / Madinat Zayed area are not in ISD and were not looked for elsewhere. The MERRA-2 result for Liwa (notebook 01) therefore stands unchecked by observations. Note that at Al Ain and Al Maktoum, the nearest long records in the UAE, MERRA-2's DP is about half the observed value, so Liwa's MERRA-2 DP is probably biased low too (an inference, not a measurement).
