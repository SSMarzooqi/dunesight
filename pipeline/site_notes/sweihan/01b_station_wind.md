## What the station winds say (written from the 2026-11-14 run, re-checked against the 2026-11-15 re-run; check the numbers above)

**Stations.** The nearest hourly station is Al Ain International (OMAL, WMO 41218), **35 km SE** of the box centre at 265 m. Its MERRA-2 cell (24.5 N 55.625 E) is also the Sweihan site cell, so the station-cell and site-cell MERRA-2 rows are the same series. Two secondary stations: Al Maktoum International (OMDW, ISD 411945-99999, not a WMO number), **50 km NNW** at 19 m, hourly from mid-2010, about 15-20 km from the Gulf coast; and Buraimi (WMO 41244, synoptic), 52 km ESE.

**Result.** The primary-station numbers are the Seih Al Hama ones (same station): DP 132 VU/yr (1.9 × MERRA-2's 70), RDD ≈ 9° against MERRA-2's 87°, S/NW mode ratio 2.5 against 0.5. But the two stations that bracket Sweihan **disagree with each other**: Al Maktoum, at the synoptic hours shared with Al Ain in 2010-2024, has DP 146, **RDD 99°** and S/NW 0.64, i.e. the MERRA-2 direction (MERRA-2 at its own cell: RDD 99°) with about twice the drift; Al Ain at the same hours has RDD 2°. Buraimi, further inland, agrees with Al Ain (RDD 341°, S/NW 2.3).

**Reading.** The strong southerly mode looks like a feature of the inland Al Ain-Buraimi plain near the mountains; the coastal station does not have it. Sweihan lies between the two, 35 km from Al Ain and 50 km from the coast, so **the station data do not tell us which regime holds at Sweihan**. Both stations do agree that MERRA-2's DP is about half the observed value (DP still low, < 200 VU, in both).

**Against the measured dunes (added 2026-11-15, from the re-run with notebook 02's result).** The dunes move toward a field-median 83°. MERRA-2's RDD (87°) is 4° from that; Al Ain's (9°) is 74° away; Al Maktoum's (99°) is about 16° away. So at Sweihan there is no heading difference for a station to explain, and the dunes side with the MERRA-2-like regime of the coastal station rather than the inland southerly one. That is consistent with Sweihan lying outside the reach of the Al Ain-plain southerly mode, but it is not a test of it.

**Published?** No. Notebook 01b's fixed rule publishes a station result only when the usable stations within the radius agree on the drift direction within 45°; here Al Maktoum and Al Ain differ by 97° at shared hours, so neither stands for the site, and no `station_wind.json` is written for Sweihan. The app shows MERRA-2 only. The DP-is-low-by-about-half finding is reported in the docs, not on screen.
